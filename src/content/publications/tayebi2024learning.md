abstract: The social awareness around the possibility of machine learning algorithms
  making biased decisions has led to an increase in Responsible AI studies in recent
  years. Algorithmic fairness is one of the concepts that should be considered when
  designing responsible AI models. The goal of these studies is to ensure the decisions
  made by machine learning algorithms in automated decision-making systems are bias-free
  and not affected by sensitive information that may lead to discrimination and consequences
  for individuals. Learning a fair representation is an effective approach to mitigate
  algorithmic bias and has been successfully applied in this domain. The objective
  of these approaches is to create representations by removing sensitive information
