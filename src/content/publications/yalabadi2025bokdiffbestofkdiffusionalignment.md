abstract: Structure-based drug design (SBDD) leverages the 3D structure of target
  proteins to guide therapeutic development. While generative models like diffusion
  models and geometric deep learning show promise in ligand design, challenges such
  as limited protein–ligand data and poor alignment reduce their effectiveness. We
  introduce BoKDiff, a domain-adapted framework inspired by alignment strategies in
  large language and vision models that combines multi-objective optimization with
  Best-of-K alignment to enhance ligand generation.Built on DecompDiff, BoKDiff generates
  diverse ligands and ranks them using a weighted score based on QED, SA, and docking
  metrics. To overcome alignment issues, we reposition each ligand’s center of mass
