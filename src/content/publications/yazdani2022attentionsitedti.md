abstract: In this study, we introduce an interpretable graph-based deep learning prediction
  model, AttentionSiteDTI, which utilizes protein binding sites along with a self-attention
  mechanism to address the problem of drug–target interaction prediction. Our proposed
  model is inspired by sentence classification models in the field of Natural Language
  Processing, where the drug–target complex is treated as a sentence with relational
  meaning between its biochemical entities a.k.a. protein pockets and drug molecule.
  AttentionSiteDTI enables interpretability by identifying the protein binding sites
  that contribute the most toward the drug–target interaction. Results on three benchmark
  datasets show improved performance compared with the current state-of-the-art models.
