# Design System: ScholarOS

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Journal"**

A ScholarOS site reads like a well-edited journal page: white paper, one ink-dark voice for text, one accent color, and hairline rules doing the work that boxes and shadows do elsewhere. Authority comes from typesetting rather than decoration. Names, titles and long reading are set in a serif; interface and metadata in a clean grotesque sans. Tabular numerals keep dates and counts aligned. Structure is announced by headings, meta lines and 1px rules, never by colored panels or cards stacked on cards.

The system is quiet so the research is loud. Density is moderate: generous vertical rhythm between sections (56–104px), tight scannable rows within them (paper lists, news, talks, posts, CV entries, alumni). Motion is limited to small acknowledgements: a link underline that draws in, a row tint on hover, an arrow that nudges 4px, the mode toggle's half-disc turning. All transitions stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the hero video stays on its poster.

The public theme ships as `journal` (code id `editorial`, the default in `config/site.yml`); `classic` remains as a frozen legacy theme with its own type scale (see Classic theme, at the end). The admin is deliberately neutral: a plain tool that shares the journal's tokens (accent, IBM Plex Sans, rules) without its serif titles or editorial patterns. The journal is one system with many shapes: personal and lab sites, light and dark, LTR and RTL, and any owner-chosen accent or font pairing all read as the same journal. Everything the owner configures (accent per mode, font families, hero backdrop, image shape, home blocks) is a variable inside this language, never an exit from it.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Serif for identity and reading (Source Serif 4), sans for interface (IBM Plex Sans), mono for code, BibTeX and package names (IBM Plex Mono).
- One accent per mode, owner-selectable, used sparingly for links, primary actions, the active nav underline, venue short-names, numerals and the brand mark.
- Rules, not boxes: 1px hairlines separate content, 2px Ink rules mark open-card top edges, aside tops and active tabs.
- Flat surfaces. Depth comes from tint and tone; the only shadow is on floating admin UI.
- Light and dark modes; dark is the journal at night, in one of three owner-selectable palettes: Lamplight (the default), Ink Well or Galley Proof. Ink line-art plates invert with the mode.
- 1080px content measure, 680px reading measure, 32px gutters (16px on phones).
- Any language: interface strings come from one table, dates follow the site's locale, and Arabic-script sites drop tracking, uppercase and italics.

## Colors

The palette is near-monochrome: cool blue-black ink on white, a ladder of four text grays, two rule weights, two pale tints, and one accent. Dark mode mirrors the same roles in one of three night palettes the owner picks.

### Primary

- **Journal Navy** (accent, a deep slightly-violet navy): links, primary buttons, the active nav underline, the rotated-square brand mark, venue short-names, research and year numerals, BibTeX toggles, note outlines' text, and the 2px focus ring. **Owner-selectable** through `colors.light.primary` (and `colors.dark.primary` for dark) in `config/site.yml`; the override is emitted per mode at build time.
- **Navy Pressed** (accent-hover): hover on primary buttons. With a custom accent it is derived as `color-mix(in oklab, accent, black 25%)` in light mode and `color-mix(in oklab, accent, white 35%)` in dark mode.
- **Navy Wash** (accent-border) and **Selection Wash** (selection): the outline of notes ("Oral", "Spotlight", "Best paper"), the BibTeX copy button's hover border, `::selection`, and the light button's hover on the contact band. Both follow an owner-chosen accent: in light mode `color-mix` with white at 75% and 84%, in dark mode with the night palette's Paper at 60% and 72%.

### Neutral

- **Ink** (ink): headings, names, titles, primary text, the outline-button stroke, open-card top rules and table header rules.
- **Body Slate** (body): secondary copy, nav links at rest, taglines, bios, the role line under the hero name.
- **Muted Slate** (muted): intro paragraphs, descriptions, excerpts, authors.
- **Caption Gray** (caption): dates, meta lines, captions, section labels, footer text. The lightest gray allowed for text.
- **Code Ink** (code): BibTeX and inline-code text on Hover.
- **Rule** and **Rule Soft**: dividers. Rule for section and list edges, Soft for dense lists (alumni, timeline rows, list-variant paper rows, writing entries).
- **Rule Strong** (rule-strong): the hero email's resting underline, a step darker than Rule so it reads as a link on touch screens.
- **Tint** and **Hover**: figure frames, venue and entry tiles, project cover plates, portrait grounds (Tint); row hover and code blocks (Hover).
- **Paper**: the page.
- **Contact Band** (band, band-text, band-muted, band-rule): the single inverted region, the "Get in touch" footer on the home page only. In dark mode its Light button hovers to Accent Hover, because Selection Wash under the band's dark label fell to 1.4:1.
- **Danger**: admin only: destructive button text, field errors, the error toast's fill, a status row that needs attention, and deleted diff lines.

### Dark mode

The journal at night, under the same roles. Toggled by `defaultTheme: light | dark | system` and the header's mode toggle. The owner picks one of three night palettes with `editorial.night` in `config/site.yml` (the admin's Settings and the CMS offer it as a select). The build writes it to `data-night` on `<html>`; editorial.css holds one token block per palette, and Lamplight is also the fallback when the attribute is missing. The palettes differ only in token values and the ink-plate filter: every component, rule and band keeps the same structure in all three.

Each palette sets eleven primitives (the tables below) and the plate filter. ### Admin

The admin's palette is the journal's light palette read through `--adm-*` aliases at the top of `src/admin/admin.css` (ink, caption as muted, rule, tint, hover, accent, accent hover, each falling back to the journal value), plus admin-only values that are not theme tokens because the admin never goes dark:

- **Paper** and **On Ink** (`--adm-paper`, `--adm-on-ink`, both white): the page and modal surface; text on the Ink and Danger toasts.
- **Toast Link** (adm-on-ink-link): links and the Dismiss control on a toast.
- **Selected** (adm-selected): the current item in pickers and the selected sidebar entry.
- **Warn** (adm-warn-bg, adm-warn-rule): notice banners, the draft status chip and the dashboard's "Suggested" chip.
- **Danger Wash** (adm-danger-bg, adm-danger-rule): error banners and deleted diff lines.
- **Add** (adm-add-bg, adm-add-ink): added diff lines. The only green in the system, and only inside a diff.
- **Scrim** (adm-scrim, Ink at 45%): behind modals.
- `--adm-float` is the Admin float shadow and `--adm-touch` the 44px phone target (see Elevation and Components).

### Named Rules

**The One Voice Rule.** There is exactly one accent hue on any screen. No secondary brand color, no teal-plus-navy pairing in the same mode, and no colored section backgrounds.

**The Caption Floor Rule.** No text is lighter than Caption Gray in light mode or dark-caption in dark mode. An owner-chosen accent must also clear 4.5:1 against Paper in its mode; in dark mode the build lifts it until it does, measured on the chosen night palette's Hover.

**The One Band Rule.** Only one inverted region exists per page: the contact band at the bottom of the home page. Heroes, sections and callouts stay on Paper; affiliations ride the hero's role line (`.ed-affil`), never a band of their own.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Source Serif 4 (with Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (with ui-monospace, monospace)

**Character:** A literary serif in a medium weight (500) paired with an engineered sans. The page feels edited and typeset rather than designed. The serif's italic carries taglines, venues and related-paper lines. Families are owner-selectable through `fonts.families` in `config/site.yml`; these are the defaults, loaded with `font-display: optional` so a late swap never shifts the page. A replacement serif must provide an italic and 500/600 weights. Every stack, in both public themes, runs: the Latin face, a Latin-only fallback (Georgia for serif, Helvetica Neue for sans), `--font-script`, then the generics. With `optional`, the fallback is what a cold load shows, so Latin names in an Arabic-script site stay serif in the serif slot instead of dropping into the Arabic face.

### Named Rules

**The Serif Speaks, Sans Serves Rule.** Names, page/paper/post titles, venues, quotes and reading text are serif. Navigation, buttons, metadata, labels, form fields and compact card headings are sans. Never set long reading in sans or interface in serif.

**The Tabular Dates Rule.** Every date, year, count and statistic uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so columns of numbers align.

**The Joined Script Rule.** Arabic-script languages (`ar`, `fa`, `ur`, `ps`, `ckb`, `sd`, `ug`) get no letter-spacing, no case transform and no italics: one rule in `src/styles/global.css`, shared by both public themes, resets all three, because tracking and synthetic italics break joined letterforms. Uppercase labels and italic taglines simply set upright and untracked there. The same languages set `--font-script` (Vazirmatn, Geeza Pro, Noto Naskh Arabic, Segoe UI, Tahoma), the slot both themes' stacks place after the Latin fallback (Georgia / Helvetica Neue), so Latin text keeps its family on a cold load while Arabic letters, which those faces lack, fall to a real Arabic face. Vazirmatn is fetched only for these languages (`FontStyles.astro`); the rest are system faces.

**The Heading-Is-The-Label Rule.** Nothing sits above a heading. There are no eyebrow or kicker labels over titles. Uppercase tracked text survives only where it _is_ the heading (`h2` section labels: research Software, people groups, project status groups, positions and join groups, CV sections, contact and repository groups). What a kicker used to carry (role, category, owner, tags) lives in a meta line under the heading: the role line under the hero name, the 14px Caption meta line, bylines.

## Layout

- **Measure:** content max 1080px with 32px inline padding (16px under 640px); articles max 680px plus gutters; intro paragraphs max 680px; bios 580px.
- **Vertical rhythm:** pages pad 64–80px top and 80–104px bottom (48px/64px on phones). The home hero pads 96px/88px (56px/48px on phones). Sections stack with 32px internal gaps and 56–96px between sections.
- **Grids:** fluid `auto-fit` grids with `minmax(min(260–300px, 100%), 1fr)` for Join's cards, software and projects; `auto-fill` with 200px minimum for people. No fixed column counts. The hero is a wrapping flex row (text 520px basis, figure 420px basis up to 500px) with a 64px gap that stacks at narrow widths.
- **Date columns:** news, talks and positions are rows with a right-aligned tabular date column (112px; 96px for alumni years) beside the content. On phones the date moves above the content and aligns to the start. The home news list's 76px column aligns to the start at every width, so a year-only date ("2024") lines up with "Sep 2024" instead of reading as a missing month; classic's home news list does the same. When the first home news item has an image it leads instead of taking a row: its plate in a 4:3 Tint frame (152px; 112px on phones) beside a Caption date, a 19px serif title and a three-line Muted excerpt. Ink plates invert with the mode like every other plate.
- **Breakpoints:** the design breakpoint is 640px. The person profile's portrait-plus-text grid (260px + 680px) stacks at 760px. Everything else is intrinsic: flex-wrap, clamp() and min().
- **Home order:** `homeBlocks` builds it per mode. Personal: hero, publications, research, updates. Lab: hero, research, publications, updates. Publications and updates follow `homepageSections` order; News and Blog share one "updates" row placed where the later of the two sits. Research, when configured, follows the papers on a personal site and the hero on a lab site.
- **Direction:** logical properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline`, `inset-inline-*`, `border-block-*`, `text-align: start/end`) everywhere, the admin included, so RTL mirrors without overrides. The hero pattern's fade origin, the phone nav's scroll mask, the link underline's draw origin and arrows are the only explicit RTL rules.
- **Owner text:** everything the owner writes (names, titles, authors, venues, bios, captions, prose) carries `dir="auto"`, so a title in another script keeps its own bidi order. Zero-specificity `[dir=auto]` rules align it to the page's column edge (left in LTR, right in RTL) except long-form `.ed-prose`; any component's own alignment wins. Roman numerals sit in `<bdi>`.
- **Language:** every public interface string comes from `src/lib/ui-strings.ts`, overridable per id under `ui:` in `config/site.yml`; no theme hard-codes English. Dates go through `formatShortDate` and `formatNewsDate` with the site's `lang`, so month names and order follow the locale.

### Named Rules

**The Shared Edge Rule.** Every row rule sits on the column edge. A row's hover tint may bleed 12–16px past the column (negative inline margin, 6px radius), but its divider does not: it is drawn as a 1px centered gradient at `calc(100% − 2 × bleed)`, or on the unbled list item. Rules from different lists, heads and sections all start and end on the same vertical line.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Public pages use no shadows at all. Depth comes from tone: Tint frames, tiles and cover plates sit one step off Paper, Hover tints a row, and the single inverted contact band closes the home page. Layering is expressed with 1px rules and 2px Ink top edges.

The hero backdrop (`hero.type`) is the one atmospheric layer, and it stays behind the paper:

- **pattern** (and **animation**, which the journal draws as the configured pattern): an Ink hairline pattern (grid by default; dots, diagonal, waves, hexagons) at 7% opacity (10% in dark), masked to fade from the figure side toward the text and the fold.
- **image**: a cover image under an 86% Paper wash, with an optional separate dark-mode image.
- **video**: muted, looping, under the same wash; autoplays only when motion is welcome, otherwise the poster stays.
- **none**: paper only.

Mask colours are not paint. The `#000` in the pattern's radial fade mask (and in the phone nav row's 28px edge-fade mask, same idea) only sets mask opacity: black means fully shown, `transparent` means hidden. It never reaches the screen as a colour, so it is not a palette token and is not undocumented colour.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Admin float** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgb(21 25 43 / 0.12)`): only for floating admin UI that overlaps content.

### Named Rules

**The Paper Rule.** If it isn't floating above other content, it has no shadow. Cards, tiles, buttons, plates and heroes are flat.

## Shapes

Small, almost-square corners. Buttons, tiles, code blocks, rectangular portraits and the mode toggle use 4px. Hover regions, figure frames, software cards and admin fields use 6px. Notes and inline code use 3px. Admin modals use 8px. Nothing is pill-shaped; the only round forms are an owner-chosen circular (50%) or oval (40%) portrait and the mode toggle's disc. Section and project cards are open shapes: a 2px Ink top rule, no side or bottom borders. Rules are 1px except the 2px Ink card tops, article aside tops, and active tab and nav underlines. The brand mark is a 10px Accent square rotated 45°.

Icons are drawn, not typed, when they stand alone: the arrow is a 16px SVG with a 1.5px round-capped, round-joined stroke in `currentColor`, matching the mode toggle's 1.5px circle.

## Components

### Buttons

Rectangular, solid and plain.

- **Shape:** gently squared (4px), 44px minimum height.
- **Primary:** Accent fill, on-accent text (white; the night palette's Paper in dark), 15px/500 sans, 0 20px. Hover deepens to Accent Hover. No transform or shadow.

### Links and arrows

- **Hit areas:** small text links (the hero email, paper titles, meta links, the footer index, contact links) reach 44px with block padding cancelled by an equal negative margin, so the target grows without moving the line; wrapping rows of them sit 22px apart so targets never overlap.
- Inline links are Accent with no underline at rest. The underline draws left to right over 0.25s on hover and focus. Links inside prose are underlined at rest (1px, 3px offset).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** separate content with 1px Rule hairlines and reserve 2px for open-card tops, aside tops and active underlines.
- **Do** keep every row rule on the column edge while the hover tint bleeds past it.
- **Do** put role, category and tag information in a meta line under the heading.
- **Do** set every date, year and count in tabular numerals.
- **Do** draw standalone arrows with the 16px, 1.5px round-stroke Arrow; keep inline → ↗ ← as typeset text in the link.
- **Do** ship line art as ink on transparency and let the build mark it for dark-mode inversion; keep photos as photos.
- **Do** keep text at Caption or darker in light mode and dark-caption or lighter in dark mode.
- **Do** give every interactive element a 44px minimum target and the 2px Accent focus ring.
- **Do** use logical CSS properties so RTL sites mirror cleanly, in the admin as well.
- **Do** take every interface string from `ui-strings` and format every date with the site's locale.
- **Do** keep the header to six links with Contact pinned, and list the rest in the footer index.
- **Do** keep the admin neutral: sans headings, ruled rows, the journal's accent and rules, persistent error toasts.
- **Do** stop all transitions under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:

- **Don't** put an eyebrow or kicker label above a heading. Uppercase tracked text is only ever the heading itself.
- **Don't** use gradients, glows or colored section backgrounds. The hero backdrop stays an Ink hairline pattern or an image under a Paper wash.
- **Don't** use pill-shaped buttons or tags, or radii above 8px (circular or oval portraits and the mode toggle's disc excepted).
- **Don't** put shadows on public-page elements. Shadows are for floating admin UI only.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue. A night palette brings its own accent in place of the light one, never beside it; no neon.
- **Don't** use emoji or icon-font glyphs as icons.
- **Don't** letter-space, uppercase or italicize Arabic-script text.
- **Don't** bring serif titles, numerals, tiles or open cards into the admin.
- **Don't** set navigation, buttons or form fields in the serif, or long reading text in the sans.
- **Don't** let configurable options (hero type, home blocks, accent, fonts) introduce a layout that exists only in one mode. Personal, lab, light, dark, LTR and RTL share every component.

## Classic theme

`classic` is the frozen legacy theme. It must keep working, it gets fixes rather than new design, and it is measured against its own scale, not the journal ramp above. It is built from Tailwind utilities on `src/styles/global.css` and the shared components in `src/components/astro/`.

- **Type:** Roboto for interface and reading, Roboto Slab (400) for names and headings, Roboto Mono for code; the same `fonts.families` override applies. Scale: hero name 30px, 36px from `sm`, 44px from `lg` (`classic-display`); page titles 24px to 30px (`classic-headline`); every home h2, About included, is one 20px heading over a 1px Rule with a 14px "view all" link (`classic-section`), and About has no tint band; body and intros 16px at 1.625, max 68ch (`classic-body`); interface, meta and lists 14px (`classic-ui`); dates, excerpts, badges (`.pub-badge`) and labels 12px (`classic-label`).
- **The 12px Floor Rule.** No classic text is smaller than 12px. Badges and labels that were 10px are 12px; pinned items say so in words, not with an emoji.
- **Container:** one 930px content container with 16px inline padding (24px from `sm`) for the top bar, header, hero, sections, page shell and footer. There is no reading measure beyond `max-w-[68ch]` paragraphs.
- **Home order:** the journal's `homeBlocks`, with About as its own section after the proof. Personal: hero, publications, research, About, updates; lab: hero, research, publications, About, updates. About on a personal site carries `editorial.bio` (the bio both themes share), else `about.text`. Research is a ruled list of area links (title 14px/500 Ink over a two-line Caption description, side by side from `sm`), and News and Blog share one updates row (two columns from `md`). News rows are 44px links whose source puts the title first, so the link's name starts with it; the date is drawn first.
- **Featured:** featured papers carry a drawn primary star before the title ("Featured" for screen readers), and the publications heading carries a 12px ★ Featured legend when any are present.
- **Hero:** the name in Roboto Slab, the role line under the name (16px/500), the research focus (16px, 18px from `sm`), one quiet 14px row of scholarly ID text links (Google Scholar, ORCID, GitHub, when configured; other profiles stay in the footer) in primary, underlined on hover like the home's Cite and View all links, each followed by a screen-reader "(external link)", then the actions: a filled Primary (View publications on a personal site, Explore research on a lab site), an outline Prospective students when a position is open, and an outline to the contact page or email. Both 44px tall, 14px/500, 4px radius. An optional 128px to 176px portrait sits at the end; without one, the actions stack in the end column from `md`.
- **Hero backdrop:** a pattern fades out toward the hero text's start edge (mirrored in RTL) and draws its grid lines at half the surface-200 rule contrast, so the name and role never sit on full-strength lines; waves keeps its own wave mask.
- **Top bar:** shown on lab sites, with their admissions and campus links. A personal site hides it when the home hero's role line already says the affiliation.
- **Phones:** only the header is sticky. The menu is MobileNav, a native modal `<dialog>` (the page behind it inert, Escape closes it) fed by `splitNav`: the header's links with Contact last, then the pages the header leaves out.
- **Icon buttons** in the header (search, mode toggle, menu) have 44px hit areas drawn at a 32px footprint (a 44px box with a 6px negative margin). The mode toggle cycles light, dark, system, its icon (sun, moon, screen) showing the current mode.
- **Search:** a native modal `<dialog>` on the `dm-*` tokens in dark; no results shows the query and a row of 44px page links to browse instead.
- **Publications rows (home):** the title in Ink 14px/500 (primary on hover, with an underline), an author line with the owner in bold Ink, then a meta line led by the venue, the type after a middle dot as caption text, and PDF, Code and Cite links (Cite goes to the paper's row on /publications, where its BibTeX copies).
- **Cards:** PostCard and FeedItem make the whole card clickable through the title link's stretched `::after`, so the link's name stays the title.
- **Colors:** classic keeps its own Tailwind palette (classic-primary navy, classic-ink, classic-body, classic-caption on white; the `dm-*` charcoal set with a cyan accent in dark). It shares no tokens with the journal.
- **Shared with the journal:** the header and footer use `splitNav` (six links, Contact pinned, the rest in a footer row with a visible "More pages" label; the copyright is one translatable string with the site name and ScholarOS in `<bdi>`), interface strings come from the same `ui-strings` table and `ui:` override, and dates use the same locale-aware formatters. Arrows mirror in RTL.
